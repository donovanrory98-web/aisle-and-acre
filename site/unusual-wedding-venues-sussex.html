Questions couples ask

Which something different are licensed for ceremonies?

Devil's Dyke Farm and Plumpton Racecourse say they are licensed for civil ceremonies. The others don't state a licence on their websites, so ask before you book.

Which something different have rooms on site?

Devil's Dyke Farm have somewhere for you or your guests to stay.

Can you bring your own caterer?

These venues use in-house or approved caterers, or don't say on their websites.
