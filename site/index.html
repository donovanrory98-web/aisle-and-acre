Questions couples ask

Is Aisle & Acre free to use?

Yes. Aisle & Acre is free for couples, and enquiries go straight to the venue you choose.

Where does your venue information come from?

From the venues themselves. We read each venue's own website and brochure, note the date and link the source on its page. Venues can confirm or correct their details at any time.

What does "ceremony licence" mean?

It means the venue says it is approved for civil ceremonies, so you can marry legally and celebrate in the same place. Registrars from the county council attend licensed venues.

Can venues pay to change what you say about them?

No. Venues can correct facts and add details they confirm, but they can't buy a better write-up. Any featured placement is always labelled.
