Privacy notice

What happens to the details you send us, in plain English.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect your names, email address, wedding date, guest numbers and any message. When a venue contacts us we collect the venue name, a contact name, an email address and a message. We don't use cookies or tracking for advertising.

Why we collect it

To pass your enquiry to the venue you chose and to reply to you. We rely on your request to do this.

Who sees it

The venue you enquire with, and Netlify, which hosts this site and processes form submissions for us. We don't sell or share your details with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Twelve months from your enquiry, then we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time through the contact form on our page for venues. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Saved shortlists

Your shortlist is stored only in your own browser. We never see it.
