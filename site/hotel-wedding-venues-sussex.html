Questions couples ask

Which hotels are licensed for ceremonies?

Pelham House and East Sussex National say they are licensed for civil ceremonies. The others don't state a licence on their websites, so ask before you book.

Which hotels have rooms on site?

Harbour Hotel Brighton, Pelham House, Horsted Place, East Sussex National and South Lodge have somewhere for you or your guests to stay.

Can you bring your own caterer?

East Sussex National let you bring your own caterer or suppliers. The rest use in-house or approved caterers, or don't say.
