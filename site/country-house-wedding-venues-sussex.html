Country house wedding venues in Sussex

A country house wedding usually means grounds for the photographs, a choice of rooms for each part of the day and, often, beds for your guests. Here is what each Sussex house offers, in their own words.

The venues

Country house · Pulborough

Bignor Park

A private estate between Petworth and Arundel with eleven acres of gardens, a Greek Loggia for outdoor ceremonies and a converted stable block for…

  • Up to 200 guests
  • Ceremony licence
  • Exclusive use

Country house · Buxted

Buxted Park

An 18th-century Palladian mansion near Uckfield, now a hotel, with ceremonies for up to 120, evening parties for 200 and 43 bedrooms for guests.

  • Up to 200 guests
  • Rooms on site
  • Exclusive use

Country house · Midhurst

Cowdray

The Cowdray estate at Midhurst offers two wedding settings, Cowdray House and the Walled Garden, with house stays, a B&B and cottages on the estate.

  • Capacity on request
  • Rooms on site

Firle Place

A historic country house in its own parkland near Lewes, made over exclusively to your party, with civil ceremonies inside or out and menus from its…

  • Capacity on request
  • Ceremony licence
  • Exclusive use

Questions couples ask

Which country houses are licensed for ceremonies?

Bignor Park and Firle Place say they are licensed for civil ceremonies. The others don't state a licence on their websites, so ask before you book.

Which country houses have rooms on site?

Buxted Park and Cowdray have somewhere for you or your guests to stay.

Can you bring your own caterer?

These venues use in-house or approved caterers, or don't say on their websites.
