Sussex wedding venues with rooms on site

Rooms on site change the whole shape of a wedding: no taxis at midnight, breakfast with everyone the next morning. These Sussex venues have somewhere to sleep.

Venues with rooms on site

  • Southend BarnsSleeps up to 22 adults and 2 children: the Milking Parlour suite, a shepherd's hut and bell tents (April to September)
  • Buxted Park43 bedrooms, with group rates for wedding guests
  • CowdrayCowdray House stays, a B&B and self-catering cottages on the estate
  • Leonardslee Gardens HouseTen ensuite bedrooms sleeping 20, for Friday and Saturday nights
  • Rathfinny Wine EstateFlint Barns B&B and self-catering accommodation, with new huts and rooms planned
  • Herstmonceux CastleA bridal suite in the castle, and rooms at Bader Hall nearby with 10% off for wedding guests
  • Harbour Hotel BrightonHotel rooms, with a wedding-night room for the couple included
  • Devil's Dyke FarmA four-bedroom farmhouse and a log cabin on site
  • Amberley CastleA Premier Deluxe room for the couple; the number of rooms for guests is not stated
  • Pelham House36 individually styled bedrooms, including a bridal suite with a free-standing bath
  • Horsted PlaceDouble rooms and suites, from Horsted and Victorian suites to the Royal and Master suites
  • East Sussex NationalHotel rooms on site; the number is not stated on the weddings page
  • South LodgeHotel bedrooms on site; the number is not stated on the weddings page
  • Long Furlong BarnNew on-site accommodation, for guests of the event only
  • Fitzleroi BarnThe Hayloft, on site
  • Selden BarnsEight rooms and the Gatehouse sleep 24 on site, with space for 22 more on request

Venues without rooms, and where to stay instead

  • Haylands BarnNone on site; the White Horse, about a mile away, has rooms
  • Michelham PrioryNone on site; the venue suggests nearby hotels
  • Southlands BarnNo rooms on site; a glamping site next door, and shuttle buses to hotels within 15 minutes

Ask about rooms

These venues don't mention accommodation on their websites: Gote Barn, Bignor Park, Bolney Wine Estate, Kingscote Estate, Plumpton Racecourse, Wadhurst Castle, Firle Place.

Questions couples ask

Which Sussex wedding venue has the most rooms?

Of the venues we have checked, Buxted Park has the most, with 43 bedrooms at group rates for wedding guests.

Can we stay the night before the wedding?

Leonardslee Gardens House includes Friday and Saturday nights for 20 guests in its wedding weekend. Elsewhere, ask the venue.
