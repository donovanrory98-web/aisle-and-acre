Exclusive-use wedding venues in Sussex

Exclusive use means no other wedding, no day visitors and no hotel guests wandering through your photographs. Here is who offers it in Sussex.

Exclusive use, or close to it

  • Southend BarnsYes, every wedding is exclusive use
  • Bignor ParkYes, one wedding per weekend
  • Buxted ParkExclusive use of the whole house is available
  • Leonardslee Gardens HouseYes, exclusive for the whole weekend
  • Michelham PrioryYour choice: exclusive use, or shared with day visitors until 5pm
  • Harbour Hotel BrightonExclusive hire of the Marine Room
  • Devil's Dyke FarmYes, on 340 acres of private land
  • Plumpton RacecourseYes, exclusively yours for the day
  • Amberley CastleYes, through the exclusive-use package
  • Firle PlaceYes, the house is made over exclusively to your party
  • Horsted PlaceAn exclusive-use package is offered
  • Long Furlong BarnDescribes itself as an exclusive wedding venue
  • Fitzleroi BarnYes, from the day before the wedding
  • Southlands BarnYes, with every package
  • Selden BarnsYes

Ask about exclusivity

These venues don't say on their websites: Haylands Barn, Gote Barn, Cowdray, Bolney Wine Estate, Kingscote Estate, Rathfinny Wine Estate, Herstmonceux Castle, Wadhurst Castle, Pelham House, East Sussex National, South Lodge.

Questions couples ask

Which Sussex venues are exclusive for the whole weekend?

Bignor Park hosts one wedding per weekend, and Leonardslee Gardens House is exclusively yours from Friday to Sunday.

Is exclusive use always more expensive?

Often, but not always. Some venues, like Michelham Priory, let you choose between exclusive use and sharing the grounds with day visitors until 5pm.
