List your Sussex wedding venue

Couples arrive on your page already knowing your capacity, your ceremony licence, your catering and your rooms. The enquiries you get are the ones that fit.

How a verified listing works

  1. We send you what we have. Your page as it stands, built from your own website and brochure.
  2. You correct or confirm it. Reply with changes, and add anything couples should know up front.
  3. Your page goes live as venue-verified. Verified venues are marked, and enquiries come straight to you.

What every listing includes

  • A full venue page with your ceremony, reception, catering and accommodation details
  • Enquiries sent to you with the couple's date and guest numbers
  • A place in every relevant style, area and guide page
  • Comparison with nearby venues on the facts couples care about

A verified listing is free. Venues can correct their facts, but can’t pay for a better write-up or a different place in our lists.
