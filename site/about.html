How we check venues

Every fact on Aisle & Acre comes from the venue: its own website, its brochure, or the venue confirming it to us directly.

Where the information comes from

For each venue we read its own website and brochure and record what it says about capacity, ceremonies, catering, rooms, exclusivity, finish times and the season. Each venue page links to the source and shows the date we checked it, currently 11 October 2026.

What we don't do

We don't copy from other directories, guess at missing details or fill gaps with assumptions. When a venue doesn't say something, the page says so and suggests a question to ask instead.

How we keep it current

Every week we check our ceremony details against the government's list of venues approved for civil marriages and civil partnerships, published on GOV.UK. Venues on that list show a note on their page with the date of the list. Every month we also look at each venue's own website for changes. A person reviews every change, and nothing on a page changes until it has been checked against the venue's own words.

Venue-verified pages

We send every venue the page we have built and ask them to confirm or correct it. Pages a venue has confirmed are marked as venue-verified.

Independence

Venues can correct facts but can't pay for a better write-up or a different order in our lists. If we ever offer featured placement, it will always be labelled.

Spotted something out of date?

Venues change their spaces and policies. If something on a page is wrong, let us know and we will check it with the venue.
