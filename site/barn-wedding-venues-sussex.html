Questions couples ask

Which barns are licensed for ceremonies?

Fitzleroi Barn, Southlands Barn and Selden Barns say they are licensed for civil ceremonies. The others don't state a licence on their websites, so ask before you book.

Which barns have rooms on site?

Southend Barns, Long Furlong Barn, Fitzleroi Barn and Selden Barns have somewhere for you or your guests to stay.

Can you bring your own caterer?

Haylands Barn, Gote Barn and Southlands Barn let you bring your own caterer or suppliers. The rest use in-house or approved caterers, or don't say.
