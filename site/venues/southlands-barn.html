Southlands Barn

A restored 17th-century barn on a working farm near Pulborough, with soaring oak beams, a private lake with views to the South Downs, and ceremonies indoors or under the pergola.

  • GuestsUp to 170
  • CeremonyLicensed
  • FoodIn-house or your own

Check your dateVisit the Southlands Barn website

The setting

Southlands Barn is an exclusive-use venue on a working farm, seven minutes from Pulborough station. The fully restored 17th-century barn is filled with fairy lights, with soaring oak beams and skylights, and looks across a private freshwater lake to the South Downs.

Ceremonies

Civil ceremonies for up to 150 take place in the Sussex Barn or outdoors under the Pergola.

Receptions

The wedding breakfast is in the Cattle Barn, and the Sussex Barn turns into the evening party, for up to 170 guests in total. There is also a courtyard and a pool table room.

Staying over

There are no rooms on site, but a glamping site sits next door and shuttle buses run to local hotels and B&Bs within a 15-minute drive.

What the venue includes

  • Exclusive use with every package
  • Sussex Barn, Cattle Barn, Pergola and Courtyard
  • Prepping Parlour for getting ready

Good to know

  • Bar and music finish at 11pm; everyone leaves by midnight.
  • Weddings run all year round.
  • A fee applies for caterers off the recommended list.

Southlands Barn: questions couples ask

How many guests can Southlands Barn hold?

Ceremonies for up to 150; up to 170 guests in total for dining and the evening.

Can you get married at Southlands Barn?

Fully licensed for civil ceremonies indoors (the Sussex Barn) and outdoors (the Pergola).

Is there accommodation at Southlands Barn?

No rooms on site; a glamping site next door, and shuttle buses to hotels within 15 minutes.

Can you bring your own caterer to Southlands Barn?

By package: recommended caterers, any caterer, or the in-house caterer Terra at Tottington Manor.

Is Southlands Barn exclusive use?

Yes, with every package.
