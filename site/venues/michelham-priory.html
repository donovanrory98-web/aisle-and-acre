Michelham Priory

A historic priory run by Sussex Past, with an Elizabethan Great Barn for up to 130, a Tudor house, and ceremonies indoors or in the orchard and gardens.

The setting

Michelham Priory sits at Upper Dicker, near Hailsham, and is cared for by Sussex Past. Weddings use the Elizabethan Great Barn, the Priory Chamber in the Tudor house, and gardens including an orchard and a kitchen garden.

Ceremonies

Civil ceremonies can be held indoors in the Great Barn or the Priory Chamber, which takes up to 60, or outdoors in the Orchard, the Kitchen Garden, on the Barn Lawns or the lawns in front of the house.

Receptions

The Great Barn seats 130 and takes 175 standing, with heating, round tables, chairs, a sound system, lighting and festoon lights. Drinks and canapés can be served in the Priory itself.

Food and drink

Couples choose from the approved caterers list. You can bring your own drinks for the daytime without corkage; in the evening the in-house bar takes over.

What the venue includes

  • Great Barn with tables, chairs, heating, sound and lighting
  • Fairy and festoon lighting
  • Bar structure, power, toilets and overnight parking
  • Night security
  • Gardens and lawns for ceremonies, drinks and photographs

Good to know

  • Hire runs Friday to Sunday.
  • Last orders 10.50pm, music off 11pm, carriages 11.30pm, site closed at midnight.
  • Bryony Basden is the wedding coordinator.

Michelham Priory: questions couples ask

How many guests can Michelham Priory hold?

130 seated and 175 standing in the Great Barn; 60 in the Priory Chamber.

Can you get married at Michelham Priory?

Licensed for civil ceremonies indoors (Great Barn and Priory Chamber) and outdoors in the gardens.

Is there accommodation at Michelham Priory?

None on site; the venue suggests nearby hotels.

Can you bring your own caterer to Michelham Priory?

Approved caterers; your own drinks during the day with no corkage, then the in-house bar.

Is Michelham Priory exclusive use?

Your choice: exclusive use, or shared with day visitors until 5pm.
