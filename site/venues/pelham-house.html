Pelham House

A 16th-century town house hotel in the middle of Lewes, licensed for ceremonies in its Panelled and Terrace rooms, with 36 bedrooms a short walk from the party.

  • GuestsOn request
  • CeremonyLicensed
  • FoodIn-house

The setting

Pelham House is a 16th-century town house in Lewes, now a hotel with walled gardens. Smaller weddings suit the original Panelled room; the Georgian Terrace room takes larger parties.

Ceremonies

Both the Panelled room and the Terrace room are licensed for civil ceremonies.

Staying over

There are 36 individually styled bedrooms. The bridal suite is in the original part of the house, with a free-standing bath.

Food and drink

The head chef writes a seasonal menu, using fresh local produce where possible.

What the venue includes

  • Panelled and Terrace rooms
  • Seasonal menu from the head chef
  • Bridal suite

Good to know

  • Guests can walk to their rooms after the party.
