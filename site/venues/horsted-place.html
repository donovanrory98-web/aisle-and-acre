The setting

Horsted Place is a country house hotel near Uckfield known for its Victorian Gothic architecture and landscaped gardens. The venue says its history is beloved by royalty.

The spaces

Weddings use the Library, the Garden Room and a Victorian-style summerhouse in the grounds.
